Engineering Reliable AI Systems

A Neurosymbolic Approach

Naveen Ashish
University of California, Berkeley (MIDS Program)

Artificial intelligence has entered an era of remarkable capability, yet reliability, trustworthiness, verification, and controlled reasoning remain open challenges. This book develops a neurosymbolic perspective on reliable AI systems, integrating knowledge representation, knowledge graphs, retrieval, reasoning, memory, verification, and architectural design principles for high-consequence domains.
